# Catalog-first creator onboarding (proposed)

**Drafted:** 2026-10-09. This is the no-code launch-MVP journey specification, not an implemented Studio flow. It begins with creator-controlled material and works without Amazon API approval. Prompt-first blank creation remains an optional entry. Every channel belongs to one creator and ultimately becomes a separate branded Vega app under that creator's Amazon Developer account; entering an Amazon identity does not create a connection or listing.

## Wizard journey and acceptance states

| Step | Creator-facing action and clear outcome | Guardrail and future owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Start channel | Sign in, name the brand and choose **Import my catalog** (default) or **Start from an idea**. Select video, audio or writing so the next prompts fit the work. Show that this project and its eventual app/source belong to this creator. | **P2** adds account, project ownership, private data and migration from local P0. No shared viewer app. Prompt-first may create a draft with no catalog claim. |
| 2. Identify source | Enter a seller/author name, storefront/author URL or ASIN as an optional **clue**, or choose **Upload CSV/JSON**, **Connect my HTTPS feed**, or **Add my works manually**. Explain in plain language: “A public Amazon link helps identify a work; it does not connect your account or permit copying.” Never ask for Seller Central/KDP/Audible passwords. | **P3** implements creator file/feed ingest and a bounded optional match review. Amazon connection stays marked unavailable until provider approval, seller consent and TV use are verified. A source begins `discovered`; a name/URL/ASIN cannot advance it to `verified`. [Feasibility matrix](integrations/amazon-catalog-feasibility.md). |
| 3. Confirm ownership and rights | Choose which works you control, which fields may be imported, and whether you can display descriptions/covers on TV. Record who owns or licensed video, audio, narration, music, artwork and excerpts, plus territory/term if relevant. Show **Needs review** wherever evidence or scope is missing. | **P2** stores evidence privately with creator-scoped access; **P3** records source permission and field provenance. A creator file assertion is reviewed; an Amazon source needs provider authorization. Rights unknown or revoked keep assets ineligible for delivery. |
| 4. Import and match | Preview rows before import: title, type, external ID, origin and proposed changes. Let the creator accept, edit, skip or merge possible duplicates. Preserve the original source record and creator edits separately. Confirm the imported count and any rejected rows. | **P3** validates/normalizes CSV, JSON and feed records into CatalogSource/CatalogItem, stable source-scoped IDs and provenance. Never silently merge equal titles or ASINs across accounts or editions. Importing a book/listing creates inventory, not playable TV content. |
| 5. Curate | Choose featured items and arrange a series, playlist, album or bookshelf. Rename/describe entries without changing the source record. New source items wait for review; missing items receive a grace/review state. Show what is still incomplete for a useful channel. | **P3** owns Collections and creator overrides; **P6** later honors field ownership during sync. A source update may propose changes but cannot overwrite a creator-owned field or automatically publish a new work. |
| 6. Attach playable media | Add an owned/licensed video, trailer, audio sample, reading, interview or companion clip and matching TV-safe artwork. Confirm separate display and playback rights; show a clear **Not ready for TV** state for missing/unsafe media. The creator may keep an inventory item unpublished. | **P2** provides private media storage; **P3** handles upload/URL validation and rights records. Device-compatible playback and public delivery are later gates. Product listings, Kindle files, Audible streams and Amazon images cannot silently become media assets. |
| 7. Generate channel | Select a suggested channel shape, then generate a draft from reviewed items and cleared media: video programming, audio listening or an author bookshelf with readings/interviews. Offer simple brand, order and text edits plus optional “Describe a change” assistance. | **P4** grounds a real supported non-mock ChannelAgent in the approved catalog and applies only bounded validated ChannelSpec mutations. The creator's manual overrides survive regeneration. `ChannelSpec` remains presentation truth; catalog/provenance remain separate. |
| 8. Preview and revise | Open a 16:9 TV Preview, use virtual remote or keyboard for focus, Select and Back, inspect detail and play an eligible sample. Display specific missing-rights/media issues beside the draft. Save and return later. | **P4** qualifies a usable Studio Preview and actual provider change; **P5** later delivers approved immutable manifests to a TV app. Preview is draft evidence, not device, publication or Appstore evidence. |

## Recovery paths that keep progress possible

| Situation | Creator-facing response | State and follow-up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Several works match a name/URL/ASIN, or the same title has editions | “Choose the work and edition you control.” Show identifying details and allow **None of these** or manual entry. | Keep candidates `discovered`; no automatic claim, merge, Amazon copy or TV publication. P3 records the chosen source/ID and review decision. |
| Empty catalog or no match | Offer a starter CSV/JSON example, manual first item, or prompt-first draft. Ask for one owned work and one licensed TV sample to make Preview useful. | A blank catalog is a valid draft; do not invent Amazon items or falsely call it imported. P3 reports zero accepted rows with reasons. |
| Amazon integration unsupported, denied or consent revoked | State that the connection is unavailable and explain the specific known reason. Offer creator-owned CSV/JSON, HTTPS feed or upload immediately. | Do not request passwords, scrape, or label a discovered source “syncing.” P3/P6 suspend affected imports on revocation. Existing creator edits remain; rights-dependent media stays out of new public revisions. |
| Feed/file errors or duplicate rows | Show the row, field and repair action; let the creator correct and retry without losing accepted work. | P3 validates format, identifiers, URLs and file safety. Re-import uses stable IDs and reviewable diffs, not append-only duplicates. |
| Unsafe, unreachable or unlicensed media/artwork | Explain whether the problem is source, rights, format or reachability. Allow replacement with a creator-owned sample or leave the item unpublished. | No playable Preview promise for that item; no deliverable MediaAsset or published manifest reference until the proper TV right is cleared. P3 handles safety/rights; P5 validates publication. |

## Three synthetic, rights-safe starting scenarios

- **Video:** The fictional *Northlight Field Films* uploads a CSV of three independently produced shorts and an owned trailer with licensed soundtrack/art. Generate a “Featured films” rail and detail/playback for the cleared trailer. A film without TV rights stays in inventory for review.
- **Audio:** The fictional *Juniper Sessions* supplies a creator-controlled JSON album list, an original sample recording and self-created cover art. Generate albums and listening rails with playback/Back. An Audible listing can be an optional matching clue only; no Audible stream is used.
- **Writing:** The fictional *Mira Vale Stories* supplies a controlled HTTPS feed of titles and an authorized recorded reading plus cover art. Generate a bookshelf, author introduction and readings/interviews. The book listing is context; the licensed recording is the playable TV experience. No Kindle text or Amazon cover is copied.

These examples describe intended product behavior only. They do not assert existing uploads, licenses, Amazon access or live TV qualification.

## Precise phase handoff

- **P2 hosted platform:** authenticate creators; enforce account/project/deployment/media isolation and global app identity uniqueness in storage; keep private proof and tokens out of exports and TV manifests; handle backup, deletion and migration. P1 contract parsing alone does not verify ownership or consent.
- **P3 catalog import:** implement creator CSV/JSON, HTTPS feed and uploads before Amazon adapters; preserve original values and external IDs, surface ambiguous matches, validate rights and safe URLs, support empty/error states and manual curation. Mark Amazon paths unavailable until both source authorization and intended downstream TV use are documented.
- **P4 channel generation:** map reviewed CatalogItems/Collections/MediaAssets into a useful ChannelSpec for each creator type, use one real non-mock provider for grounded validated/persisted changes, and prove interactive Preview. Do not let AI infer rights or import private/affiliate program content.
- **P5 live delivery:** publish only creator-approved, compatible, TV-safe manifest revisions with licensed media; keep draft separate, and validate installed-app refresh/fallback. **P6** later reconciles permitted feeds with creator overrides and review queues. **P7/P8** own creator Console submission, physical Fire TV and actual Appstore approval evidence.
