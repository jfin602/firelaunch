# FireLaunch Feature Ideas

Deferred from hackathon P0 unless promoted through docs review.

- Multi-platform export: Fire OS, Roku, Apple TV, Android TV.
- Hosted content ingestion and transcoding.
- RSS/podcast/YouTube/CMS import connectors.
- Live channels and EPG.
- Authentication, subscriptions and entitlements.
- Advertising and sponsorship blocks.
- Analytics and viewer funnels.
- Team workspaces and approvals.
- Component marketplace.
- Fully automated Appstore update submission where official APIs support it.
- AI-generated channel artwork with rights/provenance tracking.
- ADBT-powered continuous TV UX/performance diagnostics.
