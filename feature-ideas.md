# FireLaunch Feature Ideas

Updated 2026-10-09. Features promoted into the launch MVP belong to [the canonical roadmap](docs/roadmap/mvp-roadmap.md); promotion is a planning decision, not a completed implementation.

## Promoted from P0 deferrals into P1–P8

- Creator accounts, per-creator authentication and hosted persistent catalog/project storage (P2).
- Limited rights-checked catalog ingestion: creator-controlled CSV/JSON files, HTTPS feed and media uploads (P3).
- Catalog-aware, video/audio/writing-specific channel generation (P4).
- Remote immutable published manifests and installed-TV live content refresh (P5).
- Safe background sync, field overrides, review queues and permitted Amazon connectors if approved (P6).
- Guided independent creator-owned Appstore publication, limited SaaS billing, support, privacy/export/deletion (P7).
- Real creator pilots, physical-device proof and production operations/polish (P8).

## Deferred beyond launch MVP

- Fire OS/Roku/Apple TV/Android TV and other TV platform exports.
- General podcast/RSS/YouTube/CMS connectors beyond the P3 creator-controlled import/feed.
- General hosted transcoding as a full media production service.
- Live linear channels and EPG.
- Viewer authentication, subscriptions, IAP/entitlements, advertising and sponsorship networks.
- Deep viewer analytics and funnels.
- Team/multiuser workspaces and approvals.
- Component marketplace.
- Automated Developer Console submission/update via official APIs if/when supported.
- AI-generated channel artwork with rights/provenance management.
- ADBT-powered continuous TV UX/performance diagnostics.
